# Balance (#87)

Status: agreed 2026-09-16 (#87), the recommended option taken in every question of section 8. It is stage C's
plan from here, and the check that `foundation.md` section 2 is applied as a set. No runs in it.

## Why

A world holds its balance through many factors at once, and a law added alone meets a world without its
counterweights. Stage C has added section 2's rows one experiment at a time and kept or rejected each by the kinds
held on three seeds, and the runs show what that does:

- dry air alone closed every body in every medium (e066) until breath priced the closed body in the water (e067);
- a sensor block is not selected (e070): nothing worth seeing differs within a body's reach;
- cold alone was a uniform tax on the land (e071): nothing on land rewards an open body, and the day, not the
  place, made the cells cold.

Rejecting laws one by one also makes the world depend on the order they were tried in. This draft looks at the
whole: for every axis a body can vary along, where it wins and where it loses; which cycles do not close; the
scales of the differences against a body's; and which counterweights are missing. The missing laws are then built
together and searched as a combination.

## 1. The world a body lives in today

e070's world (c1225, s = 1/16, the water's layers, dry air 0.004, breath 0.01, senses through sensor blocks), second
half of seeds 9-11:

- 8,100-8,700 bodies, about a third each on land, at the surface and on the bottom. A body dies at a median age of
  about 140 steps; a grown body ends 22-28 cells from its birth over a path of 31-37.
- Deaths: hunger 69-72%, thirst 21-26%, suffocation 1-4%, broken 3-5%. Moves blocked 33-45%, children with no room
  24-33%. Kills are 10-13% of what bodies eat.
- The day is 60 steps and the year 20,000. At a census the land under the bodies spans -6 C (p10), 18 C (median)
  and 65 C (p90); the land's mean goes from 7 to 38 C over a year, and the land's bodies from about 1,900 to 4,000.
- Standing on seed 9: grass 7,500 (grazed), wood 67,000 (eaten by nothing), algae 3,400, litter 79,000. Fire
  burns 0.2% of the land a year.
- Three kinds by birth form on every seed, all at the shore: a roaming plant eater of density 1, a plant eater that
  stays and a dense roaming mixed eater. One lineage holds 67-76% of the grown bodies.

## 3. Cycles

- **Matter** is conserved in total (a drift of 1e-13), but not between places. With bodies the land gains 13-14% of
  its matter from step 10,000 to 100,000 and the sea's soil loses 10%, about 1,700 a 1,000 steps, the same on every
  seed (from the ledger: the sea's soil is the total less the land, the sea's food, the dead and the bodies). Without
  bodies the land lost 1.1% in 17 years (e062). The channel is not measured; the likely one is the bodies at the
  shore eating in the water and spending on land. Over a long run the sea's soil, and its algae, keep falling.
- **Water**: the climate's cycle closes (e061). A body's water is a state: drinking takes nothing from the world and
  drying gives nothing to the air, so water is free wherever a body touches it, and the sea is endless.
- **Heat**: the climate's only. A body has no heat; e071 charged energy against the cell's temperature of the moment.
- **Wood**: the largest stock of food in the world, nine times the grass, eaten by nothing. It shades grass, rots and
  rarely burns.

## 4. Scales

A difference parts forms only if a body cannot average it: wider than a life's travel (#68 rule 1), and slower
than a life if forms are to follow it by place.

| difference | its scale | a body's | parts forms? |
|---|---|---|---|

## 5. The counterweights, as sets

For each one-sided axis, the law that gives the other side a place, taken from the real world and written about a
material or the world. Each set is built with the laws it balances, never alone.

**A. Heat (replaces e071's cold).** A body holds heat. Its blocks make heat as they burn their upkeep; each open soft
face passes heat to or from its cell by the difference, and a hard face and the fat slow it; the body's temperature
is its heat over its mass, so a heavy body changes slowly. Under a band around its working temperature the body pays
energy to warm; over it the body pays water to cool, through its open soft faces. Real world: the surface and volume
of a body, sweating, blubber, a whale's slow cooling.
Gives the land two sides: in cold places closed, big and fat bodies win; in hot places open and small ones, which pay
water, so hot and wet land favours open bodies and hot and dry land is a conflict that dry air already prices. A big
body averages the day and a small one feels it (the size axis). Water and wood's shade are cool refuges.

**B. Wood as food.** A wood cell gives its matter only to a bite with more force behind a hard tip than the wood's
hardness (e010's rule applied to a plant; `foundation.md`'s wood row). Real world: bark, nuts, browsing.
Gives hard blocks a use and the land a second food apart from grass, in forests, which differ by place.

**C. Fat has a weight and a size.** Fat adds to a body's mass (moves, the clock, the shove), and the most a body can
hold per unit of mass is read from its genome, as e069 read it, alone of e069's three values. Real world: stored fat
is carried.
Gives fat a price where bodies move and a gain where winter or cold makes shortfalls (A and the year).

**D. The sea does not quench thirst.** A body drinks from standing fresh water (pools) and from wet ground in
proportion to its fill; the sea gives breath's water layer but no drink. Real world: salt.
Takes away the free shore: land bodies need fresh water, which is patchy (pools are 0.3% of the land) and wet ground,
which differs by place and season; water bodies need never leave the sea. It gives sight something that differs
within reach. Risk: e040's bodies sat on the water and did not travel to it.

**E. Light.** A sensor block sees as far as the light allows: the day's light, less in deep water and under wood.
(`foundation.md`'s light row.) Gives sight a time (day) and a place (open ground), with B's forests and D's water as
things to see.

**F. Height.** Moving onto a higher cell costs the mass lifted times the rise (`foundation.md`'s height row). With A,
heights are cold; with D, a trip to water over a ridge costs. Gives light, strong bodies the hills.

**G. Matter back to the sea.** Water running off the land carries a share of the soil it crosses into the sea (e035's
carrier, reused). Closes the land-sea cycle against the bodies' pump, so neither side drains over a long run.

Not in the first set: fire's damage to bodies (fire burns 0.2% of the land a year with bodies, too little to part
forms), and #34's kinds of matter.

## 7. How the set is built and judged

- **Build** A-G together in one crate from e070, each as a rate (0: off), so that e070 is the set at zero.
- **Scale** each rate before any run with a dry run on e070's censuses, as e071's `dryrun.py` did, so that every law
  starts where it bites.
- **Search** the rates together, as stages A and B did: about 20-30 sampled combinations, one seed, 50,000-100,000
  steps; keep the region where the world holds more kinds kept to a place; then three seeds around it; then longer.
  No law is kept or rejected alone.
- **Judge** by kinds by birth form held at every census; by kinds that keep to a place (a medium or a band) rather
  than the shore; by stability: the world returns after a disturbance (a kind halved comes back) and two kinds invade
  each other while rare (#72); and by the cycles: the land's and the sea's matter hold.
- **Cost**: a candidate of 100,000 steps is about 35 minutes on one core; 22 candidates on the Mac's 11 cores take
  about 1.2 hours, and with seeds and a second round about 3-4 hours. A body's heat adds one pass over its blocks a
  turn, as dry air does.

## 8. Decisions (agreed 2026-09-16)

1. **The first set**: A-G together. Fire's damage to bodies and #34's kinds of matter come later.
2. **Heat's price when hot**: water, so that heat opposes dry air on hot dry land.
3. **D, the sea does not quench**: as written. If the land empties as e040's did, the softer form (drinking takes
   water from the pools and the ground) is the fallback, tried inside the same search.
4. **The matter channel**: measured first, with a column for what bodies eat in the water and spend on land, before
   G's rate is set.
5. **Stability**: a kind halved at step 50,000 and watched for 20,000 steps, on the chosen candidates only.

## 9. What e072 settled (2026-09-16)

The sets were built and searched as section 7 asked (`experiments/e072_balance`, #88). Every rate 0 is e070
exactly; 22 combinations were searched on seed 9 and the centre of the four best run on seeds 9-11.

**Kept, by the user's decision (a).** The sets are stage C's default world, and stage C is judged from here
by the kinds at a census and the kinds that keep to a place. The rule written before the runs asked for more
kinds *held at every census* and that number did not move (3.00 against 3.00), but it counts the identity of
the kinds over six censuses, which an even world loses for being even: at a census the sets hold 5.78 kinds
against 3.44, the leanest census of the six holds 5, 4 and 6 against 3, the largest kind holds 19-32%
against 55-65%, and 2, 2 and 1 kinds keep 90% of their bodies to one medium where the controls have none.

**The axes of section 2, re-read.** Open or closed, hard or soft and the fat now have two sides: the land's
bodies close (0.64 open soft faces a block against 0.87), arm (11.9% hard against 3.7%), grow (32.4 blocks
against 26.3) and read a larger store from the genome (6.82 against the constant 5.00), while the water's
stay open and soft (1.24, 1.5%). Where it lives is no longer one-sided: every seed holds a kind that keeps
to the land, and on two of three that kind carries a tooth and takes 13-17% of its food from kills.

**What did the work.** The heat places a kind (rank correlation +0.68 over the search) and the sea that does
not quench fills the land (thirst is 60% of the deaths, the land's crowd nearly doubles). The runoff halves
the matter pump (+6.5 to +7.3% of the land's matter over 90,000 steps against +13-14%) without closing it.

**What did nothing.** Wood at `wood_food` 0.04 is 0.1% of what bodies eat: a cell's standing wood is eaten
out in a few thousand steps and grows back over 20,000, so the tooth that appears is for other bodies. The
climb is too small to show against a body that travels 25 sub-cells in a life. The heat still does not part
the temperature bands (5-7% of the land's bodies in the cold band, as in the controls), because it reads a
cell's temperature at the moment and the day swings it by tens of degrees (e071's lesson).

heat reading the cell's temperature averaged over a day. Both on this default world, searched together.

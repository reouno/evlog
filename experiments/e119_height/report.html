e119: does a height the wood can stand turn the thicket into woods and open ground?

Experiment report - 2026-11-15 - rung 2 of #135 corrected: 120 years of plants on the 16 km map of islands, terrain seed 23, one run read beside e118's

TL;DR

e118's land stayed a thicket because a number in the genome stopped a tree's height. With a height that is what the wood can stand, the same sowing makes three times the wood within 30 years: crowns stand over 23.5% of the land, 19% of its cells are closed woods and 46% open ground, and the tallest tree is 57.0 m. Four of six lines pass: no floor is dark, rain does not place the wood. Kept for rung 3.

1. Question

Animals will live among these plants. In e118 the land was a thicket under 10 m for 500 years, though old trees held wood for 16 m: a height was the least of three things, one of them a number in the genome. We took the number out and expected, in 120 years of the same sowing:

  1. It stands with every ledger closed.
  2. Crowns stand over 20-70% of the land.
  3. Old trees are tall: half of those that have stood 50 years are over 12 m, the tallest over 20 m.
  4. A wood's floor is dark: under closed woods the field holds under half of the open ground's.
  5. Open ground remains on a fifth of the cells or more, and it burns more than the wooded ground or is colder.
  6. Water places wood: the lowlands' wettest third holds 1.5 times the driest third's or more.

2. The world

e118's world with one law changed (Figure 1, blue). The field lives on 5 m cells; a stand thinned to one stem a cell is a tree, a thing with a place and a crown. The same 256 genomes are sown in year 10; a genome keeps its height number and nothing reads it.

Runs. One run, main, on Ubuntu, six threads, terrain seed 23, 100 minutes: 10 bare years, then 120 with plants. It is read at year 130, its maps as the mean of years 121-130, beside e118's main. We read:

  • ground under crowns - the share of the ground under the trees' discs; 0 is no tree.
  • closed wood, open ground - a 125 m cell with crowns over half of it or more; one with crowns over less than 5%.
  • field - all that is not a tree: grass and stands of many stems.
  • what the wood could stand - 4.2 m at a kg, by the wood's fourth root.
  • sown genome - one of the 256 of year 10; its trees are a line.

3.1 Three times the wood within thirty years, and then level

The wood is 0.93 kg a m2 thirty years after the sowing and stays: 1.03 at year 130, three times e118's, which was still rising. Only the tallest tree goes on growing. A year of plants takes 49 s against 44: taller trees did not cost more.

3.2 The land parted into woods and open ground; no floor is dark

The land has parted, where e118's trees were spread thin. Woods stand in the warm south and on the heights; the lowlands north of 59 N are 8.4% under crowns. Under closed woods the field holds 0.87 kg a m2 against 1.38 on open ground: crowns over 80% of a closed wood's ground take 25% of its light.

3.3 Open ground is the cold; rain does not place the wood

Open ground is first the cold: 79% of the cells under 9 C are open. Fire lowers the wood, 0.70 kg a m2 where 2% or more burns a year against 2.26 where nothing burns, but leaves most burnt cells wooded. Rain does not order the lowlands, because the wet ones are the cold north; within the two northern bands the wet third does hold more, x1.4 and x3.4.

3.4 A height follows its wood, and two tall lines hold most of it

Trees now lie on the curve. Trees of 50 years hold 128 kg of wood and stand 13.4 m. Two sown genomes are tall and hold 69% of the wood with 35% of the trees; two stay low, 99% of their trees under 12.1 m, with 44% of the trees. 11.3% of the trees fall a year, and what was born after the sowing is 2.4% of the living mass.

4. Discussion

The genome's limit bound e118's wood, not the climate or the time: on the same map the same sowing, without it, makes woods. What bounds the wood now is a tree's life and its leaves. A tenth of the trees fall a year, so few grow old and a wood holds 3 kg of wood a m2. A tree of 9 m carries 3 kg of leaf on a crown of 31 m2, so the floor keeps its light.

For animals neither is a fault: open ground with a field to graze on nearly half of the land, closed woods on a fifth, and food under the trees too.

Holds under one sowing of 256 genomes, one terrain seed, 120 years, nothing grazing. Not built: soil and water finer than 125 m (the woods' straight edges), trees born off the 5 m grid, a fire front that is round.

5. Conclusion and next step

Yes, yes, yes, no, yes, no. A height that is what the wood can stand turns the thicket into woods and open ground within 30 years. The floor that is not dark and the rain that does not place the wood are left as they are. Next is rung 3: animals and their minds on this land, built in stages, its design agreed first.

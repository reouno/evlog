e110: bodies in metres and seconds, and bodies that meet

Experiment report - 2026-10-06 - rung 3's pilot on the islands (#123): three runs of 70 years, bodies sown in year 40

TL;DR

Bodies now live in metres and seconds and meet by one law of contact. The machinery works: ledgers close, catches and broken faces happen every year. The world does not stand: the eaters strip the land to a hundredth of its producers in ten years, flesh stays under 1% of the diet, and two lines of one size hold every island. Next is the plan's turn: what holds the eaters.

1. Question

Since e106 the bodies lived on a planet, in cells and updates, with nothing to eat them; on a small world they stripped the land (e107). This pilot puts bodies on the islands with every law in metres and seconds, and lets them meet. It asks whether that world stands, not whether a law is good.

  1. The ledgers close - to 1e-9, with the carrion.
  2. The world stands - bodies alive in year 70, producers on half the land.
  3. Meetings happen - catches, failed faces and kills every year; flesh is eaten.
  4. The grain holds - a body holds 0.2-2 S.
  5. The update may be lengthened - levels within a factor 1.5 at 64 minutes and 3.2 hours.

2. The world

A body senses within a reach, steers by weighted pulls, and walks its speed times the time. A second evaluator gives each body it sees a pull and a press. A body holds about S kg of animals: a clutch is carried, then hatches as one brood.

Runs. isles1 (410 km2 of land), bodies sown in year 40, to year 70, S 4,000 kg, one seed, at three lengths of the bodies' update. A first pilot at S 400 kg was stopped in its second year at 556,000 bodies.

  • Matter - dry weight of the animals the bodies stand for.
  • Failed face - a contact where the front's pressure passed the face's strength.
  • Flesh - carrion and kills eaten, of everything eaten.
  • Founder's line - the descendants of one sown genome.

3.1 The eaters strip the land and then live on what is left

The bodies' weight rises to 75,000-128,000 t in two to four years, eating a stock of litter nothing else was taking, then the wood and the seed. What the producers fix falls by 64-86%, and the bodies settle at a tenth of their peak, taking three quarters of that. A consumer that finds food by sweeping a path has no density below which food is safe.

3.2 Bodies break each other, and nothing lives on it

Catches and failed faces are counted from the first year, so the law engages. But a kill lies in the cell for all, a gut passes 0.5% of a body's mass an update, and litter is everywhere: bodies with flesh over a tenth of their diet never hold 0.7% of the matter.

3.3 One size, two lines, every island

Of 256 founders, two are left from year 50, both on all eight islands. Bodies over 1 kg grown held up to 38% of the matter early and none after ten years: what remains is swarms of the default 0.1 kg. A body walks 450-1,900 km a year, so the sea parts nothing.

4. Discussion

The meeting law was to be the brake on the eaters. It is not, and thirty years of one seed cannot say whether hunters would arise later; they can say the land does not wait for them. The plan named this turn before the pilot: the producers' side of the cycle.

Read from the code and the outcome, not tested: dead matter is fully digestible and decays only once a year; wood and the seed bank are in reach of any body; and small bodies in swarms are fed where a large one starves.

The scale chosen in #126 assumed 8.8-20 t of animals a km2. Here it is 180-310 t at the peak and 16-48 t on a bare land, so what a body stands for is open again - after the eaters are held, since the weight depends on what holds them. The three update lengths differ, but with one run each that is not separated from chance.

5. Conclusion and next step

The bodies' machinery stays in e110's crate; nothing goes to base/ until a world stands with it. The next piece is designed from the top: what, in the real world, keeps eaters from stripping the land, and which of it this world lacks.

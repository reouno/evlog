e086: a year a body can live through

Experiment report - 2026-09-19 - stages A and B of c1225 at years of 11,880, 2,400, 1,200 and 600 steps

TL;DR

A year of 1,200 steps puts the winter inside a grown life. c1225 with its producers still passes stages A and B at that year (fire's yearly line aside), and a seasonal place is lean for 400 steps against a grown life of 500-570, where today it is lean for 3,960. A place fed in the lean month is 15 cells away. Next: the bodies on this world, with the flesh slowing in the cold (#93).

1. Question

P1 (#93) starts from a ratio: a grown body lives 1/20 of c1225's year, so no body meets a season. The year is the sun's parameter; the cheapest layer goes first. At a shorter year, does the world still stand, and how long and how far is its winter to a body?

  1. Stage A holds at a shorter year: the land follows the sun within 35 steps.
  2. Stage B holds, but fire's line per year falls with the year.
  3. The winter fits in a grown life at a year of about 1,200.
  4. A fed place is within 25 cells of most lean places.

2. The world

e062's climate and producers on c1225, every law and rate per step unchanged; only the year moves. The last year is written by month: temperature, night low and the grass's growing index.

Runs. Years of 11,880 (control), 2,400, 1,200 and 600 steps; the day stays 75. Each run spins the climate 20,000 updates and grows producers 200,000 steps; about 5 minutes each on the Mac.

  • Lean month - a land month whose growing index is under a quarter of the land's median.
  • Seasonal place - lean in 1-11 months of its year.
  • Lean spell - a place's longest run of lean months, in steps.
  • Distance - cells over land from a lean place to the nearest place fed that month.

3.1 The winter is a third of the year, at any year

At every year down to 1,200 a seasonal place is lean for about four months running, so its spell falls with the year. The lean months are the winter: nothing grows under 5 C, and a lean month averages -11 C. At 600 a month is shorter than a day, so day and season mix.

3.2 The refuge is within the legs' reach, not the eyes'

Half the lean place-months have a fed place within 9-15 cells over land, set by the terrain and not by the year. A grown body today travels 8 cells in its life: the refuge is within its legs' reach (60 steps at full speed), not within its eyes'.

4. Discussion

The world's winter is a third of its year at any year length, so the year alone sets whether a body meets it. That makes the year a lever with little side cost: the habitats and the producers' shares stay.

What the world does not give is a way through the winter. Its nights fall toward -30 C and a lean month averages -11 C; today a body under 15 C pays energy to warm itself, so a winter of 400 steps costs a body of 25 blocks about three times its upkeep, and a grown body holds 90-300 steps of fat. The bodies' side of #93 is a law of the flesh: its pace follows its warmth, so the cold slows a body and its fat lasts.

Not shown: whether bodies move toward the fed places. No sense reads the season.

5. Conclusion and next step

The year for #93's bodies is 1,200 steps: sixteen days a year, a winter shorter than a grown life, the producers as they stand. 600 loses the season in the day; 2,400 keeps the winter longer than a life. Next: stage C on this world with the cycle design in #93.

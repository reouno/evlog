e064: a body's scale of matter

Experiment report - 2026-09-14 - every matter quantity of a body times s = 1, 1/4 and 1/16 on e062's world c1225, 100,000 steps each (foundation stage C, second step, #78)

TL;DR

One factor s on every matter quantity of a body, on e062's world c1225: s = 1/4 holds 770 bodies and s = 1/16 holds 5,652, 18 times e063's. At 1/16 bodies meet, kills are 17% of the intake and each lineage lives 2-4 ways instead of one. The crowd jams, pins every body's density at its ceiling, and costs 11 ms a step. Next: choose stage C's s and build its first trade-off.

1. Question

e063's bodies lived one per 360 land cells, one way: they keep e059's economy against grass that grows a twentieth of e059's sun, and no trade-off can be judged in a world so thin. #78 multiplies every matter quantity of a body by one factor s. Written before the runs:

  1. More bodies, far fewer than 1/s: 500-900 at s = 1/4 and 600-2,000 at 1/16, since smaller bodies graze grass that grows by its own cover lower.
  2. Bodies meet more, but kills stay under 10% of the intake and each lineage lives one way.
  3. Still cheap: under 2 µs a body, and 300,000 steps under an hour at s = 1/16.

2. The world

e063's world and bodies with one number more. A body keeps its economy in its own units and s converts matter where it crosses to the world; the eye reads food in the body's units, so s changes the crowd and not what a body sees.

Runs. c1225 with draw d11, seed 9, 100,000 steps from e063's settled world, one core each: s = 1/4 and 1/16, and s = 1, which reproduced e063's files exactly. The control is the world without bodies. Every 1,000 steps we record:

  • Bodies - alive, grown (300 steps or older), and their lineages.
  • Contact - bodies pressed a step; the kills' and the dead's share of the intake.
  • Ways of living - e060's census of the grown bodies: diet, tooth, roaming.
  • The crowd - moves blocked, children with no room, density, distance from birth.
  • Grass and fire - grass standing and grown, land burnt, against the control.
  • Cost - wall time a step: the world, the bodies, the lineages.

3.1 A smaller body buys a crowd, though the grass grows less

Smaller bodies do graze the grass lower, and it grows less: 22% of the control's at s = 1/16, 42% at s = 1. But the crowd eats as much matter a step as e063's bodies: it takes two thirds of the grass that grows before it dies or burns, and eats its own dead.

3.2 The crowd meets, kills and lives more ways

Contacts per body rise 4.3 times at s = 1/16, and the kills and the ways of living rise with them. The kills are not a tooth's: 29% of the grown bodies take over a fifth of their intake from kills, and 2% of those carry a tooth.

3.3 The crowd jams, and density is its weapon

At s = 1/16, 52% of the moves are blocked and 41% of the children find no room. In the jam density is weapon and armor, and every body sits near the ceiling, paying in speed (0.19 against 0.44).

3.4 A body still costs about a microsecond

A body costs one to two microseconds at every scale; at 5,652 bodies they are 83% of a step. The lineages' detection grows with the square of the gene lists alive and is the part to watch.

4. Discussion

The mechanism in the hypothesis was half right. The grass grows less under smaller bodies, but the number of bodies followed the scale, not the grass: at 1/16 less of the grass dies to litter and fire, and more than a quarter of what the bodies eat is one another.

The crowd makes niches (#68's rule 2) and jams, as e056's did. The density ceiling of 2, e025's choice, now binds, and the crowd's kills come from density. foundation.md's first trade-off, the water's two layers, puts a price on density (a light body reaches the surface), which is where this crowd would feel it.

Not shown: c1236, other seeds, runs past 100,000 steps, a scale between 1/4 and 1/16, another density range. The random start and e063's join (land only, grass and the dead as food) were not varied.

5. Conclusion and next step

One scale on a body's matter sets the density, and at s = 1/16 stage C first holds lineages that live different ways. It costs a jam, a pinned density and 55 minutes a run, which still allows 6 seeds on 2 worlds in about an hour. Next, to agree in #78: s = 1/16 for stage C, and the water's two layers as its first trade-off.

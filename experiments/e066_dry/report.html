e066: dry air

Experiment report - 2026-09-14 - a soft block over dry ground loses water through its open faces, on e065's world c1225 at s = 1/16, 100,000 steps at two rates (foundation stage C, fourth step, #80)

TL;DR

A soft block over dry ground now loses water through every face it opens to the air; hard blocks and blocks in water lose nothing. At the stronger rate 19% of deaths are by thirst and land holds 31% of the bodies (e065 41%). But bodies answered with shape, not armor: they filled their grids and grew, in every medium, and kinds kept to one medium did not appear. Not kept; next, a price on the solid body in water.

1. Question

e065's layers made no kinds kept to one medium: a lineage at the density of water lived in all three. foundation.md's second trade-off asks for different blocks instead: a soft block facing dry air loses water, a hard one does not, and nothing is lost in water. Written before the runs, for the stronger rate:

  1. The land's bodies change their blocks (hard 10% or more, or 0.60 open faces per block or fewer); the water's stay within 0.05.
  2. Kinds by medium: 50% of grown bodies in lineages keeping 90% to one medium.
  3. Thirst thins the land: its share 15-35%, 5-30% of deaths by thirst.
  4. Grown land bodies rarely in water carry twice the hard share of those mostly in water.

2. The world

e065's world and bodies at s = 1/16, with the water's two layers. Dryness is 1 minus the ground's fill: over a year its mean spreads three times wider over the land than the air's humidity. On land a body drinks at the sea's edge; pools are 0.25% of the land.

Runs. c1225 with draw d11, seed 9, 100,000 steps, one core each, at dry 0.001 and 0.004. Control: e065's run, which this code reproduces exactly with dry 0. Every 1,000 steps we record:

  • Bodies - by medium, and deaths by thirst.
  • Blocks - hard share, open soft faces per block and size, by medium.
  • Water - a body's fill and its turns with a block in water.
  • Kinds - lineages keeping 90% to a medium; ways of living (e060) per lineage.
  • The crowd - moves blocked and children with no room.

3.1 Thirst thins the land

Land bodies hold under half their water (fill 0.46) and have a block in water in 7% of their turns. They do not crowd onto wet ground or the shore: the ground under them is drier than under e065's. They live away from the water and pay for it.

3.2 Bodies close up with the blocks they have

The loss is paid per open face, and the cheap way to have fewer is a full grid, not a shell: a 6x6 square of gut and muscle opens 24 faces for 36 blocks, and every block still eats or moves. A hard block weighs twice a soft one and does nothing else. Filling the grid makes size pay, by perimeter over area.

3.3 The land's answer spreads to the water

A solid body loses nothing in the water, so nothing stops the land's answer from spreading: the large lineages span the shore and carry it to the surface and the bottom. One lineage keeps to land (66 blocks, density 1.83); open frames keep to the surface, where openness is free.

4. Discussion

The trade-off was meant to ask for armor, but armor is the dear way to close a face: it adds weight and takes the place of a gut or a muscle. The bodies closed their faces with the blocks they had.

A trade-off priced on one side only is answered by a body good on both. In e065 each layer priced the other's answer; here only the land pays. Kinds kept to a medium need the water to price the solid body: for example a soft face that takes from the water what the body burns, so that a closed body starves there while an open one dries on land.

At 0.001 a body dries out in over a thousand turns and the land's blocks did not change; its differences at the surface, which loses nothing, are that run's path. Not shown: other seeds and c1236, longer runs, the air's humidity as the dryness, drinking from wet ground.

5. Conclusion and next step

Dry air is not kept as stage C's default, by the rule set before the runs: the land's blocks did not change apart from the water's. In this world, at these rates, it thins the land and makes bodies solid and larger everywhere, with no kinds by medium. Next, to agree in #80: a law that prices the solid body in water, run together with the dry air.

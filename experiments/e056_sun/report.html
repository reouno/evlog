e056: More sun under the clock

Experiment report - 2026-09-12 - e055's world run at one, two and four suns, seeds 9-14 (#65). Answer: no. Four times the light grows 1.3 times the plant and 64% more bodies, and the winners get smaller, not bigger. The sun stays 1.

TL;DR

e038 found a body's size capped by the income a gut takes from crowded ground, and extra sun buying more bodies rather than bigger ones. e055's clock made a big body cheap per unit of mass, so the question was worth asking again. It is still number: mass p90 falls from 46 to 38 from sun 1 to sun 4, a gut block still takes 0.0019-0.0022 of matter a step, and half the extra light falls on ground with no soil left to grow from.

1. Question

The world has one size axis, the clock (e055): a body of mass m takes (16/m)^0.5 turns a world step, so its upkeep comes to about m^0.5 instead of m. That changes the cost side of e038's arithmetic, which measured the ceiling on size as the income a gut block can take. With being big now cheap, does a richer world buy size instead of number?

  1. Size rises with the sun: the winners' blocks and the 90th percentile of the mass rise from sun 1 to 2 to 4, within a world state.
  2. Or it is still number: the bodies rise with the sun and the size does not - the ceiling is the income, and the clock did not lift it.
  3. The world stands: every winter floor above 50.

2. The world

No new law: one argument moves. The sun multiplies every cell's regrowth rate, and the rain's cap with it, so a world that burns twice as much can rain it back. What it does not multiply is the matter: the 140,139 units of this world are the same at every sun, and a cell can only grow what its own soil holds.

Runs. Pilots on seed 9, 20,000 steps, at suns 1, 2 and 4: the sun 1 pilot reproduced e055's number for number, so the sun 1 control is e055's kept batch and was not run again. The batch: sun 2 on seeds 9-14 and sun 4 on seeds 9-11, 100,000 steps. Nine runs, 30 minutes on the Mac.

  • mass p90, size p90 - the 90th percentile of a body's mass and of its blocks.
  • winner's body - blocks in the mean body of the lineage holding the most body-steps of the last third.
  • intake per gut - matter a gut block takes per step.
  • grown, shaded, barren - the light a step, split into what became plant, what the canopy held back, and what fell on soil-less ground.
  • state - hunter when more than 0.03 blocks are broken per body per step (e045).
  • kills' share, diversity - the flesh of kills in what bodies eat; the diversity number (#42).

3.1 Four times the light grows 1.3 times the plant

The sun is a rate, and it acts on the soil a cell still holds. Raising it pulls matter out of the ground faster than the world returns it: the free soil falls from half of everything to a fourteenth, and light landing on empty ground grows nothing - 2% of it at sun 1, 50% at sun 4. What leaves the soil is standing in the crowd's fat, which more than doubles.

3.2 The bodies do not grow

Nothing rises with the sun. The two worlds where the sun made the biggest difference to the winner are the two where the state changed - a hunting world grows a bigger body than a grazing one at any sun - and in the three grazing worlds run at sun 4, the winner is the fourteen-block body this world has been selecting since e043.

3.3 The crowd takes the extra food, and thins it back out

A gut block takes the same 0.002 of matter a step under one sun and under four. That is e038's result again under a law that was supposed to have changed it: what a body earns is set by how often it reaches ground that has something on it, and more light means more mouths on the same ground rather than more on each mouth.

3.4 A richer world is safer, and stops hunting

Winter floors double and then triple, and the mean age rises from 795 steps to 1,420: plenty is a buffer against the winter. Hunting goes the other way. Three of six worlds hunt at sun 1, two of six at sun 2 and none of the three at sun 4 - with prey this cheap to catch and plants this cheap to eat, the tooth stops paying. Three seeds cannot settle that last one.

4. Discussion

We raised the wrong dial. The sun sets the rate at which soil becomes plant; the world's income is the matter cycling through it, and that is fixed. Past sun 2 the extra light falls on cells with nothing to grow: the plant grown per step is 87.8 at sun 2 and 88.5 at sun 4.

What did arrive went to number. The crowd grazes every cell back to where a gut block earns 0.002 a step, so a body's income is a property of the crowd and not of the sun - e038's result, from before the clock existed. The clock made a big body cheaper to run; it did not make food easier to reach, and reach is what binds.

And plenty is not what these bodies lack: room is. Before the sun is touched, 60% of the forward moves a body tries fail because the way is taken and 29% of its children find no free cell; at sun 2 that becomes 63% and 34%. More matter would buy what this light bought. What is missing is something that thins the crowd or tells one cell from another.

5. Conclusion and next step

Not kept: the season world stays at sun 1. Two experiments have now tried to grow a bigger body by enriching the world (e038, e056) and both bought numbers instead - and the richer the world, the less it hunts: blocks broken per body per step are 0.019 / 0.022 / 0.006 at sun 1, 2 and 4. Next: fouling (#56), a worsening that falls on the body that stays put - the crowd making its own patchiness in a world where every cell is alike - and then the brain (#58).

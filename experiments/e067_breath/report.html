e067: breath in water

Experiment report - 2026-09-14 - a block in the water uses breath and a soft face open to the water gives it back, with e066's dry air, on c1225 at s = 1/16, 100,000 steps at two rates (foundation stage C, fifth step, #81)

TL;DR

In water a block now uses breath, and each soft face open to the water gives it back; e066's dry air stays on. The water's bodies opened: 1.25 faces per block at the surface against 0.78 on land. Shapes keep to a medium (85% of bodies in common shapes), but lineages still span the shore, each with an open form in water and a more closed one on land. Kept at breath 0.01; next step to agree in #81.

1. Question

e066's dry air priced openness on land only, and the land's answer, a filled grid, spread to the water: lineages kept to one medium fell to 1-11%. Here the water prices the same count the other way: a soft face open to the water breathes, so a solid body suffocates there. Written before the runs, for breath 0.01:

  1. The media part their shapes: the surface and the bottom open 0.20 or more faces per block than the land (e066 0.10 and 0.08).
  2. Kinds by medium: 30% of grown bodies in lineages keeping 90% to one medium (e066 1-11%).
  3. The largest lineage keeps 90% of its grown bodies to one medium.
  4. Breath kills in the water: 5-30% of deaths by suffocation.

2. The world

e066's world and bodies at s = 1/16, with the water's two layers and dry air at 0.004. Breath is a second fill beside the water. Water is the sea and pools; a body at the surface breathes through the water, as one on the bottom does.

Runs. c1225 with draw d11, seed 9, 100,000 steps, one core each, at breath 0.003 and 0.01. Control: e066's run at dry 0.004, which this code reproduces exactly with breath 0. We record every 1,000 steps, and every body every 10,000:

  • Bodies - by medium, and deaths by thirst and by suffocation.
  • Shape - open soft faces per block, size and hard share, by medium.
  • Breath - a body's breath and its turns with a block over land.
  • Kinds - lineages and common birth shapes keeping 90% to a medium; ways of living (e060) per lineage.

3.1 The water's bodies open up

The water's bodies became hooks, rings and lattices of gut and muscle: 96% of grown bodies at the surface and 86% on the bottom open a face per block or more (e066 23%). They breathe more than they burn (breath 0.96) and have a block over land in 3% of their turns: they do not breathe from the shore.

3.2 Shapes keep to a medium, lineages do not

By shape the media part: 85% of grown bodies in common shapes keep to one medium (e066 32%). By lineage they do not. Inside each large lineage the water's bodies open more faces and are smaller: lineage 1640 opens 0.83 per block on land at 38 blocks, and 1.19-1.24 in the water at 23-24. A lineage is joined by mates within 6 genes, and they meet at the shore.

3.3 Thirst pays, not suffocation

Suffocation took 1.5% of the deaths; thirst took more than in e066 (27% against 19%). The lineages that span the shore now carry the water's openness onto land: grown land bodies' median open faces per block rose from 0.64 to 0.85. Each medium pulls the same lineages its way.

4. Discussion

The same count priced both ways did what one price could not: shapes now differ by medium. It did not make kinds by lineage, because the lineage is not where the difference lives. Mates within 6 genes join the open water form and the closed land form, a few genes turn one into the other, and bodies of both meet at the shore, where the land holds both layers.

The price was paid in shape, as in e066, not in deaths. An open body breathes freely in the water and only the dry land prices openness, so the water's bodies opened as far as the shore lets them. The land's bodies, in the same lineages, came out more open, and thirst took more.

Not shown: other seeds and c1236, longer runs, breath without the dry air, a floating body that breathes the air, and whether the forms would part into lineages if the shore did not join them.

5. Conclusion and next step

Breath is kept for stage C at 0.01, with e066's dry air at 0.004, by the rule set before the runs: the water's bodies open far more faces than the land's. In this world it parts shapes by medium but not lineages; each large lineage holds a water form and a land form. Next, to agree in #81: counting kinds of living inside a lineage, or section 2's next row.

e118: do plants at metres make woods and open ground, placed by the climate?

Experiment report - 2026-11-14 - rung 2 of #135: 120 years of plants on the 16 km map of islands, terrain seed 23, one run read and one kept as a lesson

TL;DR

Plants now grow at metres on the 16 km map: a field on 5 m cells, and trees as things. After 120 years every ledger closes, but the land is a thicket with young trees, not woods and open ground: crowns stand over 5.7% of it. A tree's wood grows and its height does not: height is its genome's limit, and two sown genomes are 94% of the trees. Kept as it is for the next rung.

1. Question

An animal lives among plants of its own size, so the producers, cohorts on 125 m cells, are rebuilt at metres; nothing eats them yet. We expected the climate to place wood and open ground:

  1. It stands 100 years with every ledger closed.
  2. Crowns cover 20-70% of the land; wet windward ground holds three times the wood of the lee and the south's lowlands.
  3. There is a tree line.
  4. Leaves differ between north and south.
  5. The trees at year 100 are not the sown ones.

2. The world

The field lives on fine cells of 5 m. A stand of it is many stems; thinned to one stem a cell it is a tree, a thing with a place, a height and a crown (Figure 1). In year 10, 256 random genomes are sown on every cell with soil.

Runs. Two runs on Ubuntu, six threads, terrain seed 23, 90 minutes each: 10 bare years, then 120 with plants. main is read at year 130, its maps as the mean of years 121-130. dense, the first long run, packed a new tree's leaves into a narrow crown. We read:

  • ground under crowns - the share of the ground under the trees' discs; 0 is no tree.
  • light taken by crowns - the share of the land's light the trees take; the field has the rest.
  • field - all that is not a tree: grass and stands of many stems.
  • what the wood could stand - 4.2 m at a kg, by the wood's fourth root; a tree under it is held by its genome.
  • sown genome - one of the 256 of year 10; its trees are a line.

  1. Yes hypothesis 1: 120 years of plants, and the worst ledger error is 1e-10, in matter.
  2. No hypothesis 2: crowns stand over 5.7% of the ground (asked 20-70%), and the lowlands' wettest and driest thirds hold the same wood, 0.28 kg a m2.
  3. Yes hypothesis 3: by cold. Crowns stand over 9.5% of the ground at 1,200-1,400 m and 1.0% above, and over 0.1% of the lowlands north of 59 N.
  4. No hypothesis 4: the field's optimum is 12.8 C in the south and 11.9 C in the north, and it sheds 0.44 and 0.45 of its leaves a year.
  5. Partly hypothesis 5: as lives yes, as lines no. 0.6% of the trees were trees by year 30, but two of the 256 sown genomes are 94% of them.

3.1 The land is a thicket with young trees in it

The field fills the land within thirty years and then holds. What still moves is the trees' wood: it doubled in the last 60 years and is still rising, so year 130 is a transient. Stands of 0.5-5 m hold 64% of the fine cells, with trees among them: a thicket, where a closed wood with a dark floor was expected.

3.2 Warmth and fire place the trees; water does not

Trees stand where it is warm and where fire does not come: crowns are over 11.0% of the ground where the year's mean is above 15 C and 0.6% under 9 C, over 6.7% where nothing burns and 2.1% where 2% or more burns a year. Water does not place them: the lowlands' driest third gets 311 mm a year, the wettest 870 mm, and they hold the same wood.

3.3 A tree's wood grows and its height does not

Trees of over 50 years hold 304 kg of wood and stand 8.5 m, as trees of 20-50 years do with 89 kg; a stem of 304 kg could stand 17.5 m. Height stops at the genome's limit, 5.2 and 8.5 m for the two lines; wood goes on growing. A taller wood needs a taller line, and what was born after the sowing is 1.3% of the living mass.

3.4 The first long run: a narrow crown halves a new tree's light

In dense a stand counted a tree had its leaves packed into a crown a seventh of its cell, and its light halved. The run ended with 252,065 trees whose crowns stood over 0.9% of the ground; 44% had a leaf at the winter's end, against 98% in main. A tree's crown is now its height's, as the stand's was: nothing changes the day a stand is counted a tree.

4. Discussion

What bounds the wood is not the climate but what was sown and how a height is made. Two founders' lines became the trees, and 120 years is a few tree lives: too short for a taller line to be born and spread. Under crowns as thin as theirs (a leaf area of 1-2) the field keeps most of its light, so no floor goes dark and no wood closes.

The reading holds under one sowing of 256 genomes, one terrain seed, 120 years and nothing grazing. long, 500 years of the same world, is running.

Not built: soil and water finer than 125 m (the woods' straight edges), a fire front that is round (scars are diamonds), small eaters of leaves, the sea's producers.

5. Conclusion and next step

Yes, no, yes, no, and lives but not lines. Plants at metres stand, and what they make in 120 years is a thicket with young trees, bounded by two sown lines' height limits and not by the climate. The laws stay in this crate for the next rung and go to base/ when animals stand on them.

e112: the layers

Experiment report - 2026-10-27 - the ground and the crowns as places a body enters (#125): ten runs on a trial world, a pilot of 60 years on the islands and three runs beside it

TL;DR

Most of the land's food is in the ground, out of reach. The layers open the ground and the crowns. Designed bodies entered both and every line died within ten years; evolution found neither door in sixty years on the islands, and no body eats flesh. The islands showed history instead: the same world fills with 18 kg bodies, where e111's sowing gave 0.1 kg ones. Next: the catch, and the sea as a place.

1. Question

e111's land stands, but under one way of living: a 0.1 kg litter eater, no flesh, one line. 45% of the land's living producers are roots, stores and the seed bank, unreached, and a grown body reaches 12% of the leaves. Does a body that can enter the ground or the crowns live there, and does evolution find the way in?

  1. Each door can be lived in - a designed line lives in its layer to year 30.
  2. Evolution has not found a door in fifteen years on the trial world.
  3. The land is not eaten from below.
  4. With the switches off the world is e111's to the digit.
  5. On the islands a door is used and flesh is over 1% of the diet (the pilot's lines, written before it).

2. The world

e111's world with three layers a land cell (Figure 1), each behind a switch: off, it is e111's world to the digit. Three designed bodies test whether a door can be lived in: a digger of 0.1 kg, a climber of 10 mg, a hunter that walks.

Runs. A trial world of 16 km (26 km2 of land), bodies sown in year 15, read over years 26-30: the layers, each left out, and the designed bodies put in in year 20. Then the islands (410 km2), bodies sown in year 40, to year 100, against e111's run, and three runs that ask why its bodies are large.

  • The land's producers - kg of plants a m2 of land, against the same world with no bodies.
  • Matter - dry weight of the animals the bodies stand for.
  • In a layer - share of the bodies' matter in the ground or the crowns.
  • Flesh - carrion and kills, of everything eaten.
  • Ways - groups of genotypes that differ by diet, place, size, range or make (analysis/ways.py).

3.1 The ground is the land's largest larder

Roots, stores and the bank hold 0.38 kg a m2 against 0.04 in leaves. A store is sugar with almost no A or B: a fuel, not a body. The layers changed the land by less than two realisations differ; only a designed digger ate into it.

3.2 A door entered is not a door lived in

Each designed line entered its layer and died of something the door did not price. The digger's young, 0.3-6% of an adult's mass, move a metre a day and cannot dig out of the cell their kin ate down. A climber small enough for the crowns dries out between drinks.

3.3 On the islands: no door, no flesh, a land that stands

The land stays at 81-100% of the world without bodies, above the control in every decade, while the bodies hold 2.7 times its matter by year 100. Nobody uses a layer; litter is the whole diet from year 80.

3.4 The size of everything is set by who survives the first years

Three years after the sowing the world held 48 bodies, and the line that came through was a large founder's. Sown a year later it came through large again, with the clause and without it; e111's sowing came through small. The ways number 2-2.6, parted by land against sea and by size; one line holds all.

4. Discussion

A door is the price of entering a layer; living there costs more. The ground's foods are poor in A, eaten where the digger is born, and paid for at a young body's pace; the crowns are dry. Neither cost was in the design, and both are physics the world already has. A layer becomes a place when a body can live a life in it.

The islands' large bodies are the first to hold this world while litter is eaten, and they are not a law's doing: one bottleneck of a few dozen bodies decided it for sixty years. That is what a replay that differs looks like, and a warning: one pilot reads one history.

Not shown: whether the clause matters for the land on the islands (one realisation each), and whether a door is found over centuries.

5. Conclusion and next step

The layers and the clause stay in this crate, on, for the next step; nothing goes to the base. The pilot met two of #116's turns: no line lives on flesh with a place to hide (the catch itself is next to redesign), and one line holds land and sea (the sea as a place with its own cost). The open measure (#119) read the pilot and closes.

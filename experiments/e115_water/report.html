e115: does water in matter turn the eaters to the living?

Experiment report - 2026-11-05 - a gate on the trial world, two life seeds on the islands to year 60

TL;DR

Living tissue now holds water, and a mouthful brings it. Thirst eases (36% of the deaths against 44%, and 4% against 24%), and food brings 15-39% of the bodies' water. But the leaf stays about 1% of the diet, no body enters the crowns, and locks hold less than before. Food waters the wet ground, where the bodies already live. Next: the door itself, height around a plant.

1. Question

In e114 the living were 93% of the food on land and the bodies ate litter for 97.8% of their diet. A plan check found every way to the living priced in something a body cannot pay, and no food carrying water while thirst was the first cause of death.

  1. The world stands with water in matter.
  2. The living are eaten more, most where it is dry.
  3. Thirst eases.
  4. The doors open: bodies in the crowns, locks rise.

2. The world

e114's world, with one switch and one constant: 4 kg of water a kg of dry tissue that is all working part. No organ is named.

Runs. A gate on the 16 km trial world (off is e114 to the digit), then life seeds 2 and 3 on the 64 km islands to year 60, each against e114's run of the same seed, on one machine.

  • leaf of the diet - living leaf over all eaten.
  • food of the water - water in food over all a body takes in.
  • ground's fill - soil water over what it holds, where the body is.
  • locked share - producers' mass on a locked genotype.

3.1 Thirst eases; the diet does not move

Water in food takes a share of the deaths from thirst in both seeds without changing what the bodies eat. Litter stays 99% of the diet in all four runs.

3.2 The thirsty die on dry ground, where food brings little water

In seed 2, 95% of the thirst deaths are on ground under a tenth full, where bodies spend 2% of their time. On the driest tenth food brings 2% and 5% of the water (seeds 2 and 3), on the wettest 27% and 54%. A litter's water follows the ground, and the leaves that hold water whatever the ground are mostly out of reach.

4. Discussion

The piece did what it was built to do: every mouthful is also a drink, priced by what the matter is. It did not do what it was for. Eating the living pays most where it is dry, but a body there reaches a few leaves, and its matter is the dead, which are dry there too.

The doors are the binding term. A median body reaches 14% of the leaves, and none lives in the crowns where the rest hang. Water was what a climber lacked in e112. No body went up here to try again.

In seed 3, thirst is rare, and three quarters of it falls on the wettest ground, where most bodies crowd. That is the rate a crowd can drink, not dry ground.

5. Conclusion and next step

Water in matter stays in the crate, on. It eases thirst and leaves the world standing, but it does not part the diet. The living stay behind height and the crown. The next gap is the door itself: height as a continuous axis around a plant, set aside in the plan check.

e060: A census of ways of living

Experiment report - 2026-09-13 - analysis of existing runs, e010-e059 (#71). No new runs.

TL;DR

A way of living is a grown body's diet, tooth and range. Counted per body, worlds hold 1-10 ways, and the count follows the kills' share of the food (rank correlation 0.61). Counted per lineage, every world holds 1-3: the rest is spread inside one lineage. The laws read as new axes added none (0 of 6 pairs), so that reading of the series is dropped. e059 added shapes, not ways.

1. Question

vision.md made ways of living the measure and proposed a frame from ecology: the ways that coexist are at most the independent limiting factors, each with a trade-off. Before any new law, the frame is checked on the runs we have, and a one-seed count that doubted e059 is redone on every seed.

  1. The laws read as new axes (e012 places, e025 weight, e026 season and cloud, e032 winter by height) hold more ways of living than their controls.
  2. The other laws (more of one food, an axis overlapping the old one, no trade-off) hold no more.

2. The census

No new runs. Each grown body falls into one of twelve ways of living. A world's count is the ways holding 5% of its grown bodies, per body or with each body counted under its lineage's commonest way. The kills' share of the intake comes from the log.

Runs. 27 law-control pairs from e010-e059 (same code, same seeds, 1-6 seeds), at the census steps of each run's second half, plus e045, e055 and e058: 156 runs, 14 seconds on one core. Every threshold was also moved (10 settings).

  • ways per body - ways holding 5% of the grown bodies.
  • ways per lineage - the same, each body under its lineage's commonest way; a lower bound.
  • kills' share - the flesh of kills over all the world ate, second half of the run.
  • state - hunter world at 25% or more; e010-e012 have no kill log.
  • verdict - more or fewer when a seed differs by one way or more; the majority of seeds.

  1. No Axis laws hold more ways in 2 of 6 pairs per body (e025, e032, both by bringing kills), 0 of 6 per lineage.
  2. Yes No other law adds ways: 21 of 21 pairs per body, 19 of 21 per lineage (e040 thirst 0.002 and e038 sun 2 by one).

Every verdict above holds under the thresholds moved in the appendix: the frame holds on 21-24 of 27 pairs per body across 10 settings.

3.1 The count follows the kills, per body only

Worlds whose kills are under a tenth of the intake hold 2.4 ways per body; at 30% or more, 5.2. The share runs evenly from 0 to 40%, so a hunter world is a degree, not a second state. Per lineage the slope is weak (rank correlation 0.37): 1.6 ways in grazer worlds, 2.0 in hunter worlds.

3.2 Most of the count is spread inside one lineage

In e055 seed 9, the world with 10 ways, lineage 593 holds 68% of the grown bodies and leads every way. Its flesh share runs 0.22-0.65 between quartiles, one hump cut into three diets. Its toothless members were born with 14.9 muscle blocks and keep 3.9: the tooth was broken off. Roamers are 1.7 times older than stayers.

3.3 Laws move the count by moving the kills

e025 put all four seeds into hunter worlds and e028 took three of four out; e046, e051, e054, e056 and e057 moved seeds both ways, so their verdicts split. Within one state, a difference of one or two ways is the size of the scatter between seeds of one world.

3.4 e059, corrected: more shapes, not more ways

Plant eaters without a tooth are 66-95% of the grown bodies on the islands and 64-92% in the uniform world. What changed is that they stay: 38-71% against 10-32%. Hunter worlds: 0 of 6 island runs, 2 of 6 uniform runs.

4. Discussion

The frame predicted that laws adding a limiting factor would hold more ways of living. Counted per lineage, none did. Per body, weight (e025) and the winter by height (e032) did, by raising the flesh of kills, the one second food these worlds have; season, cloud and a second kind of place did not. The series cannot be read as axes added and axes missed, and that reading is dropped.

The bound itself stands untested: no world held more than three kinds, and none had more than two foods. The per-body count is the spread of a kind: members eat what lay near them, lose a tooth to a break, and range further the longer they live. A viewer sees that spread, but selection acts on kinds.

What this cannot show: a lineage joins bodies by gene lists, so the per-lineage count is a lower bound (e032 seed 3 holds a 70-block hunter and a 17-block gut in one lineage). No run records the tooth a body was born with, its kills apart from scavenging, or the path it walked.

5. Conclusion and next step

Count ways of living per kind; per body the count measures the spread of one kind. Per kind, every world so far holds one to three, and none of the laws tested added one. The frame's reading of the series is dropped; its bound stands untested. Next, #72: put a second kind into a world held by one, to learn whether a world can hold two before asking evolution to find them.

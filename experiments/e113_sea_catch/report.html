e113: the sea as a place, and the catch

Experiment report - 2026-11-01 - a gate on the trial world, then three runs on the islands to year 100

TL;DR

With a press paid for what it breaks, a hunter evolves unaided and lives 25 years beside its prey, 2.7% of the matter, without emptying the world, in one history of two. The sea is still not a place: one body lives on land and at sea. The run ends before the hunter's fate is known.

1. Question

e112 left one line on one food. Would a sea that parts islands and a catch that pays, together, change that?

  1. A hunter lives.
  2. It does not empty the world.
  3. The sea is a place.
  4. Parted islands hold different sizes.
  5. A crash stays local (added before the pilot).

2. The world

e112's world with four new laws. None names a hunter, a refuge or a size.

Runs. isles1, one seed, to year 100. all; sow41, sown a year later; hungry70, all with 50 designed hunters on the largest island in year 70. The gate had failed (a hunter emptied the trial world in a year); the user chose to run these anyway. We read:

  • Hunter - a genotype with over half its lifetime diet in kills.
  • Held - how far ways differ by island, 0 to 1.
  • Way - a group by diet, place, size, range and make (analysis/ways.py).
  • Animals - a body is a cohort of them.

3.1 A hunter evolves and stays

The first hunter is one body in year 76. Matter then doubles about every three years, and has been flat since year 97. It is the same founder's line as its prey: a frame of 0.36 and a gut of 0.13 against their 0.10 and 0.56. hungry70 shares all's first 70 years, so these are one history; sow41 has none.

3.2 The world stands; the sea does not part it

The two largest ways of all are one body at 18.4 kg: 12% at sea with a gut of 0.56, and 73% at sea with 0.65. floor moved the share of bodies at sea only from 33% to 24-27%.

3.3 A hunter on one island hits all of them, then starves

The line as written, in bodies and matter, read "no crash to test": island 1's matter never fell under 0.69 of all's. A body is a cohort, and the animals show it: at year 72, 3.9 million against 64 million on island 1, and 6.0 against 22.6 on the others. Animals fell far more than matter, which reads as the young eaten, everywhere; that was not measured directly.

4. Discussion

The designed hunter failed the gate and the evolved one lives. The designed body is a strong killer that takes its food down and starves; the evolved body arrives as one mutant among the prey it can already eat, and grows about a hundred times slower than the gate's arithmetic said a hunter could. Why was not read.

The world held the designed hunter by its starving, not by the sea. Nothing shows sight or cover doing anything, and all four laws ran together, so the hunter cannot yet be credited to one.

It does not show a food web. Flesh is 0.55%, one founder's line holds everything, and litter is still 99% of the diet. Animal counts swing tenfold in all alone, so only the first years' gap is read.

5. Conclusion and next step

The catch can be lived in, and the sea is still no place. The press's price is kept; floor, sight and cover stay on, with no effect shown. Next is the hunter's fate, which needs the run carried past year 100.

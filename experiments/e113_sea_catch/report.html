e113: the sea as a place, and the catch

Experiment report - 2026-11-01 - a gate on the trial world, then three runs on the islands to year 100, one carried to year 200 - updated 2026-11-04

TL;DR

With a press paid for what it breaks, a hunter evolves unaided in one history of two. Carried to year 200 it settles at about 1,000 t: kills fall from 0.55% to 0.18% of the diet as its prey multiply fourfold. After a century the world's size turns over, 18 kg to 0.57 kg. The sea is still not a place.

1. Question

e112 left one line on one food. Would a sea that parts islands and a catch that pays change that?

  1. A hunter lives.
  2. It does not empty the world.
  3. The sea is a place.
  4. Parted islands hold different sizes.
  5. A crash stays local (added before the pilot).
  6. The hunter's fate by year 200: settles, grows or crashes (added before part 3).

2. The world

e112's world with four new laws. None names a hunter, a refuge or a size.

Runs. isles1, one seed, to year 100. all; sow41, sown a year later; hungry70, all with 50 designed hunters on the largest island in year 70; all200, all carried to year 200. The gate had failed (a hunter emptied the trial world in a year); the user chose to run these anyway. We read:

  • Hunter - a genotype with over half its lifetime diet in kills.
  • Held - how far ways differ by island, 0 to 1.
  • Way - a group by diet, place, size, range and make (analysis/ways.py).
  • Animals - a body is a cohort of them.

3.1 A hunter evolves and stays

The first hunter is one body in year 76; its matter doubles about every three years to year 97. It is its prey's founder's line, with a frame of 0.36 and a gut of 0.13 against their 0.10 and 0.56.

3.2 The world stands; the sea does not part it

The two largest ways of all are one body at 18.4 kg: 12% at sea with a gut of 0.56, and 73% at sea with 0.65.

3.3 A hunter on one island hits all of them, then starves

In bodies and matter there was no crash to test: island 1's matter never fell under 0.69 of all's. In animals there was: at year 72, 3.9 million against 64 million on island 1, 6.0 against 22.6 on the others. That reads as the young eaten everywhere, not measured directly.

3.4 Carried to year 200: the hunter settles, its prey shrink

all200 is all to the digit for 100 years. The hunter stays at 18-24 kg, on 11-18 land masses and at sea, its share of the matter falling from 2.7% to 0.5%. The 0.57 kg way beside the 18 kg one at year 100 holds 91% of the matter by year 200, most of it at sea.

4. Discussion

The designed hunter takes its food down and starves; the evolved one arrives as one mutant among prey it can already eat, and grows about a hundred times slower than the gate's arithmetic allowed. The world held the designed one by its starving, not by the sea. All four laws ran together, so the hunter cannot be credited to one.

It is not a food web: flesh under 1%, one founder's line, litter 99% of the diet. What keeps the hunter from following its prey up, and whether it drives the turn to small bodies, are unread; a run without hunters to year 200 would part the two. e112's lesson that the first years set a world's size holds for a century only.

5. Conclusion and next step

The catch can be lived in and settles, one small chain on top of litter; the sea is still no place. press_work is kept; the other three stay on, with no effect shown. Next is a plan check on the sea and on litter, still 99% of the diet (#116).

e104: generations inside a stand

Experiment report - 2026-09-26 - the redesign's second rung (#116), again: e103's world with stands that die at the lifespan their wood sets, two runs of 300 years

TL;DR

In e103 no producer evolved: a stand never died of age. Here a stand dies at the lifespan its wood sets, and its slot is won by lottery from the seed bank. The producers now evolve: genotypes born after the sowing hold 12% of the biomass by year 300 and are still rising, and the groups double to 7.6-7.8, each keeping to its place. The line for change (H4) still fails as written: the heaviest single genotype is a founder. Next: longer, cheaper runs.

1. Question

Rung 2 asks whether many producer forms arise, hold by place and keep changing while the world stands. e103's five lines, unchanged:

  1. The ledgers close - water, A, B and the living's matter, to 1e-9.
  2. The world stands - half the land and a fifth of the sea held, biomass steady.
  3. Forms hold by place - 5 effective groups, group against place NMI 0.2.
  4. They keep changing - the leader changes 3 times in 100 years; a late group holds 1%.
  5. The living change the ground - evaporation by 10%, the soil's A:B map.

2. The world

e103's world - producers with a genome and tissue chemistry, seeds, fire, small eaters with keys - with one law and one rule changed:

Runs. c1225, sown in year 10, run to year 310, two seeds of the living, 2.3 hours each. A first batch was stopped by its rule at year 100 (mutants 7.5e-7 of the biomass: 2 seeds in 10,000 were new); a mutant is now a whole seed release, one in a hundred. Measured as e103, plus:

  • Born after sowing - share of producer biomass in genotypes made by mutation.
  • Lifespan - years a stand lives, from its wood.

3.1 The producers evolve

The mutants' share rises from 0.1% at year 60 to 12% at year 300 in both runs, faster each century, and the groups climb again from year 110-160 as mutants found new ones. The heaviest genotype stays a founder because new forms are spread over 540-550 genotypes, none large: the H4 line reads one genotype, not the change of the whole.

Both runs' mutants carry twice the defence compound, bigger seeds and a warmer optimum. Beyond that they part: run 1's live 24 years against 59 (softer wood), run 2's move growth from store and roots to leaves and seed.

3.3 The world stands and keeps its places

The land fills in a century and holds; each group keeps to its region; tall stands reach 33 m in run 1 (e103: 9 m). Transpiration still replaces the soil's own evaporation, and the runoff stays at 10%.

4. Discussion

One law turned sorting into evolution. The rate is set by the generation: stands that live decades turn a slot over a few times in 300 years, so 12% is a beginning, not a balance, and the curve has not bent. Whether new forms come to lead places, and whether keys and eaters race, needs thousands of years, and a year costs 26 seconds today, 13 of them the climate's.

H4 fails by its own measure. Evolution here makes many small genotypes, so the heaviest single one changes last; a reading of change should follow groups and their founders' dates, not one genotype. The line stands as written.

5. Conclusion and next step

With generations, producers evolve and the world holds twice the groups, by place. Rung 2 stands on this law, and its producers go into base/. Next: runs long enough to see forms replace each other - a cheaper year (the climate in threads) and a millennium.
